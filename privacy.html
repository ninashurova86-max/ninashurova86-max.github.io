Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, My Uploader requests access to your YouTube account (scopes youtube.upload and youtube) only to:

  • upload videos you created with the app to your own channel;
  • set the title, description, tags, thumbnail and privacy status of those videos;
  • read your channel name to confirm the connection.

Storage

The OAuth access token is stored only on your own computer, in a local file. No Google user data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any server operated by the developer, and it is never sold or shared with third parties.

Third-party services

Video scripts are generated with the OpenAI API and narration with Microsoft Edge text-to-speech, using only the topic you enter. No Google user data is sent to these services.

Google API Services User Data Policy

My Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and by deleting the local token file.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen of this app.
